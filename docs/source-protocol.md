# Source and review protocol

## Source hierarchy

Use primary sources in this order:

1. Statutes, regulations, official gazettes, court opinions, and official agency legal records.
2. Official DoD, service, combatant-command, LEC, and installation publications.
3. Official agency guidance, technical documents, monitoring results, and public consultation materials.
4. Secondary legal and technical analysis for discovery, context, and issue spotting.

LinkedIn posts, news articles, law-firm alerts, and trade publications may identify a development but should not be the sole evidence for a final status determination.

## Original-language requirement

For South Korea, Japan, and Germany, retain the original-language source whenever available. Record the English translation separately with:

- translator or translation method;
- translation date;
- whether the translation is official;
- unresolved terminology;
- the original-language citation.

An English summary may support operational triage, but the original text controls a legal determination.

## FGS currency review

Each country module should maintain a comparison record containing:

```text
OEBGD version → FGS version → host-nation law version → FGS incorporation status
```

The initial review cycles are:

- Korea: use the five-year update cycle stated in the current KEGS, while monitoring interim changes.
- Japan: use the two-year JEGS update cycle stated by USFJ.
- Germany: verify the current GFGS through the responsible European authority; do not treat the publicly identified 2019 copy as current without confirmation.
- OEBGD: monitor the official DoD Manuals page for new volumes and changes.

## Revalidation triggers

Create a revalidation task when:

- an OEBGD volume changes;
- a host-nation statute, regulation, standard, or official PFAS guidance changes;
- a SOFA or bilateral environmental arrangement is amended;
- an FGS is revised or its review cycle expires;
- a court or agency action changes the status or interpretation of an underlying rule;
- a translation changes a material term;
- an installation identifies a new PFAS activity, pathway, waste stream, or contractor function.

## Minimum review record

Every reviewed item should identify:

- reviewer;
- review date;
- source checked;
- current status;
- applicability determination or unresolved question;
- whether an FGS crosswalk is required;
- next review date or trigger.
